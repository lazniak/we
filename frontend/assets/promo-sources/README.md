# Promo artwork provenance

2026-10-01. Film and automation backgrounds generated with the built-in Codex image generator. Original PNGs are retained in this folder with their provenance metadata. WebP delivery versions live in public/promo. They are generic illustrations, not documentary pictures of HEXART facilities or clients. Display label: "Obraz stworzony z pomocą AI."

Composition brief: wide 16:9 cinematic photography, dark calm left 35% for the translucent upload panel; main subject at 72% width, 45% height; calm bottom for promotional copy and top-right for contact card. Warm amber side key, graphite shadows, realistic craft and materials, fine film grain. No logos, text, UI, neon or imaginary client products.

Film subject: plausible professional cinema camera on tripod in a dark soundstage. Automation subject: quiet dark server racks with plausible cables and amber practical light.

Jetson, Wojna1939 and Paul delivery assets were copied from the existing HEXART website assets under D:/code/hexart.pl/dist (casestudies/jetson.webp, casestudies/wojna1939.webp and paul-luz.webp). Their source is the existing website, not a new model recreation of the client project. The Jetson and Wojna1939 AI labels have been retained. Copy describing these projects follows the HEXART brandbook facts.

Films 2026-10-01: owner-supplied 10.125 s, 2560x1440, 24 fps H.264 originals for automation, branding, contact, ecommerce, facemapping, film, genai, heritage, history and jetson (6.5-11.5 MB each with audio). Kept locally in assets/promo-sources/video and ignored by git because of their size. `npm run promo:video` (scripts/encode-promo-videos.mjs) moves new drops from public/promo/*.mp4 there and writes silent H.264 renditions to public/promo/video: a baked-in ping-pong loop (forward, then backward without repeating the end frames, 20.2 s), 1920x1080 CRF 29 for the desktop stage and 1280x720 CRF 30 for the phone banner, plus the first frame as a WebP poster. It also regenerates components/promoVideos.ts, which switches those arts to films. Reduced motion, Save-Data, 2G and refused autoplay fall back to the poster. The films are AI-assisted and keep the AI label.
